The BSL 1.1 source license grants you rights to the **code**, not to the
project's marks. The two are intentionally separate: anyone may fork the
source, but only Craton Software Company may use the Craton marks on products, services,
or distributions.

## What is permitted (no permission required)

- **Nominative use** — accurate, factual references to the project:
  - "works with Craton HSM"
  - "Craton HSM compatible"
  - "drop-in for Craton HSM"
  - "based on Craton HSM (with modifications)"
- Using the marks in unmodified documentation, changelogs, academic papers,
  blog posts, and conference talks that discuss the project.

## What requires written permission

- Using "Craton" or "Craton HSM" (or any confusingly similar name) in the
  **product name**, **service name**, **company name**, **domain name**, or
  **package/crate name** of a fork, derivative, or competing offering.
- Using the Craton logo or any stylized Craton wordmark.
- Co-branding, certification claims ("Craton-certified", "Craton-approved"),
  or implying endorsement.
- Merchandise, SaaS branding, or any commercial use of the marks beyond
  nominative reference.

## Forks

You are encouraged to fork the source. **Rename your fork.** A fork that
preserves the `craton-hsm-*` crate names, the Craton branding in its README,
or markets itself as "Craton HSM" infringes these marks regardless of the
upstream BSL grant.
